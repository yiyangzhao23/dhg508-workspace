# Peking University and the admission of women, 1919–1920

Research on when Peking University first admitted women, and what "admitting"
meant (attending classes as an auditor vs. formal enrolment).

## Key finding

- Women were first admitted as **auditors (旁聽生)** from February 1920; the
  university's notice of 11 March 1920 lists them as such.
- They were **formally enrolled after the summer 1920 entrance examination**.
