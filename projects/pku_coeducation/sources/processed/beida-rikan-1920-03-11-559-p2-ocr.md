## Why this matters for the research question

The module is the university's own notice that women had been admitted, and it
records them explicitly as **旁聽生** — auditors, not yet formally enrolled
degree students. This is the document behind the claim that Peking University
opened its doors to women in early 1920, and it shows precisely *how* they were
first admitted.
